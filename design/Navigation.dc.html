Frame 2 — active session

Tab bar — always 4
Home · History · Exercises · Progress. Flat, equal weight, icon + label always shown. Active = volt icon + label + 2px top indicator; inactive = --muted-foreground. Templates: reached from Home, not a tab. Body + Settings: from the Home header avatar.
