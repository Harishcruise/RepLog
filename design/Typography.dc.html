Font roles
Space Grotesk Weights in use 500 · 600  (family 300–700) Screen titles, section headers, buttons, chips, table headers. Distinctive digits and the tail on the g give it a modern-tech read; still a real UI face. Tighten tracking slightly at title sizes.
Geist Weights in use 400 · 500 · 600  (family 400–700) Body copy, all inputs, captions, helper text, and exercise names. Already wired via next/font. Neutral and legible at 12–16px; holds proper nouns without shouting.
Geist Mono Weights in use 500 · 600  (family 400–600) Weights, reps, RPE, est. 1RM, volume, timers, timestamps, axis ticks. Always with tabular-nums so columns lock. Swap the whole role to Martian Mono for a harder sci-fi read.

Rules
Do Set every piece of data — weight, reps, RPE, 1RM, volume, timers — in Geist Mono with tabular-nums. Build hierarchy from size, weight and colour — Space Grotesk gives you 500 and 600. Keep exercise names in Geist, mixed case.
Don't Set exercise names or body copy in Space Grotesk all-caps — it shouts. Use Space Grotesk below 14px, or Geist below 16px for anything interactive. Mix proportional and tabular figures in one column.
